## Artwork

All textures in `pack/` are generated by `tools/gen_assets.py` in this
repository. They are original work: no portion of a Microsoft/Mojang texture,
another client pack, or a commercial asset pack was copied into them. The four
crosshair reticles, the heart/hunger/armor silhouettes, the hotbar frames, the
panel surfaces, the icon and the wordmark were all drawn for this project.

## Minecraft assets

Minecraft, the Minecraft logo and all vanilla textures are trademarks and
copyright of Mojang Studios / Microsoft. This pack is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Mojang or Microsoft.

* No vanilla texture, sound, model or font is included in this repository.
* Only layout facts were used: canvas sizes, nine-slice values, and the
  location of the crosshair cell inside `textures/gui/icons.png`.
* `tools/make_low_fire.py` reads fire textures from **your** installation at
  build time and writes the result to `build/`, which is git-ignored. Do not
  redistribute that output unless your local law and the Minecraft EULA allow
  it.
